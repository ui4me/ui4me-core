Hyphenation patterns (TeX hyphenation patterns of the hyph-utf8 project, https://github.com/hyphenation/tex-hyphen, commit 5684c0f51c0b81133db2efbe60a408b4155a3ff5), used by `hyphens: auto` through ui4me-text's own implementation of Liang's algorithm. The pattern files are embedded unmodified (three comment lines at the head are added).
